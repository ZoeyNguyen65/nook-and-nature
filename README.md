# Nook & Nature: A Mindful Study Room

## Project purpose & vision
Nook & Nature is a mindful focus dashboard designed to eliminate digital burnout by unifying fragmented workflows into a single sanctuary. It combines a high-contrast Pomodoro timer with an embedded media player and calming nature presets to reduce the cognitive load caused by tab-switching. Through soothing audio alerts, the app encourages intentional breaks to protect eye health and sustain physical energy.

The primary goal is to provide a digital sanctuary for students and professionals. By integrating time management and sensory environment controls into one minimalist interface, the application ensures that users can maintain a flow state without the clinical and distracting feel of traditional productivity tools.

## Accessing the application
To open the application, simply navigate to this link in any modern web browser: https://zoeynguyen65.github.io/nook-and-nature/

## Using the application
You can customize your own experience via the "Settings" button to adjust focus durations and nature backgrounds. Once configured, press "Start" button to begin the timer. When the countdown completes, an alert will notify you ("Time to rest your eyes!"). You can manually reset the timer or toggle ambient audio at any point to suit your workspace needs.
